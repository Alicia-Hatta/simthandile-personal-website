A little about me

More than a title. A journey.

I believe that where you begin does not have to determine how far you can go. Growth comes from staying curious, taking responsibility and being willing to learn.

My name is Simthandile Hulana, and I am known online as Hatta. I am an 18-year-old student from Umzimkulu, KwaZulu-Natal, currently studying ICT Business Analysis at Durban University of Technology's Indumiso Campus in Pietermaritzburg.

My journey combines technology, business thinking, creativity and leadership. I am interested in understanding real-world problems and exploring how digital solutions can make systems more useful, accessible and effective.

Beyond my studies, I am growing as a content creator and developing ideas through The Hulana Technologies. I know that there is still much to learn, and that is part of the journey: improving my skills, learning from people around me and turning ambition into consistent action.

This website is my digital home — a place to document my progress, share what I am building and connect my interests in technology, entrepreneurship and personal growth.
